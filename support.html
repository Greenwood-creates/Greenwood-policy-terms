Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

If you subscribed on iPhone/iPad, subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and select Quiz Wizards Pro.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and choose Delete Account. If you can’t access the app, email us from the address used to sign in.

I found incorrect content in a quiz

Some quizzes are AI-generated and may be inaccurate. Email us the quiz title (or a screenshot) and what needs correcting.

I’m having trouble signing in

If you use Apple/Google sign-in, make sure you’re using the same provider you originally used. If the issue persists, email support with your device model and iOS version.
